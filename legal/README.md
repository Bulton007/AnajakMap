# AnajakMap legal drafts

Open index.html, terms.html or privacy.html locally in a browser. Keep styles.css alongside them. These restored English drafts include the owner-confirmed identity (Nagumo), country (Cambodia), public support email (forusea168@gmail.com) and 18+ launch policy.

They are review drafts, not effective policies or a certification of compliance. Retention/deletion operations, age controls, provider details and jurisdiction-specific review remain outstanding. Publishing a preview does not enable registration or resolve these items.

For a static deployment, upload this entire legal folder and use legal as the hosting root. No build command or secrets are needed. index.html provides the home page; terms.html and privacy.html provide the two document URLs. Do not upload .env or backend credentials.

After finalizing and publishing the policies, configure the verified HTTPS URLs and approved version using AUTH_TERMS_URL, AUTH_PRIVACY_URL and AUTH_POLICY_VERSION in the private backend environment. Never substitute nonexistent or draft URLs merely to bypass the registration gate.
