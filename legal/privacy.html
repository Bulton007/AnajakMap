Privacy Policy

Draft for review — not effective. Updated 12 October 2026. Deployment, retention and deletion details must be completed before production use.

AnajakMap is operated by Nagumo in Cambodia. Support and privacy contact: forusea168@gmail.com.

Information used

  • Account name, email, password hash, verification status, session records and accepted policy version/time.
  • Travel information you provide, including itineraries, expenses, reservation records, journal entries, saved phrases and companion conversations.
  • Photos, receipts, documents and other media you explicitly choose to upload.
  • Group invitations, membership, votes and optional shared location.
  • Technical request records, including routes, response status, timings, trace identifiers and account identifiers. Hosting services may also process network addresses.

Purposes

Information supports authentication, account emails, travel record storage and synchronization, requested file processing, group features and diagnosing failures or misuse. Optional location and upload permissions can be managed through device settings.

Email and providers

Gmail SMTP is configured for account emails. Google processes recipient addresses and message contents, including verification and reset instructions. Sender credentials stay on the backend; verification codes are not returned by the authentication API.

Live AI, translation, map, storage and travel integrations may process information needed for requested features. Many current development integrations use labelled fixtures. Actual production providers, regions and relevant transfer arrangements still need documenting before activation.

Group sharing

Authorized members can access information shared through group features. The current shared-location feature expires locations after five minutes; this does not establish a deletion period for logs or backups. Other members may keep information already viewed.

Device storage and security

Native authentication credentials use platform secure storage. Offline travel records and pending edits use separate device storage and are not separately encrypted by the app. Signing out clears local credentials but can retain account-associated downloads. Clearing device or browser storage can remove unsynchronized work; it does not delete server data.

Access controls reduce the risk of unauthorized access, but no security system can guarantee protection against every incident.

Retention and deletion

Verification and reset codes expire after 15 minutes. Email queue cleanup removes expired or sent records on subsequent worker runs. Account, travel-content, uploaded-file, log and backup retention schedules are not yet finalized.

Signing out and uninstalling do not delete your account. The reviewed authentication API has no account-deletion endpoint. A verified request and deletion process must be implemented before it can be described as available. Direct privacy questions to forusea168@gmail.com.

Younger users

The initial service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Report concerns about a younger user's information to the privacy contact. Age eligibility controls and handling of such reports still need implementation.

Outstanding details

Before publication as an effective policy, complete the hosting/provider inventory, processing purposes and applicable legal bases, retention/deletion schedules, relevant rights and request procedures, effective date, policy version, and any required postal contact address. Advertising and marketing practices also need confirmation. Obtain jurisdiction-specific review.
